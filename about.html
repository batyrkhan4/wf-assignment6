Frequently Asked Questions

🔎

    MCTiers League is an official platform ranking Minecraft PvP players across various combat tiers (HT1, LT1, HT2, LT2) and organizing high-stakes seasonal tournaments.

    The championship officially tests and ranks Sword 1.9+, Axe & Shield PvP, End Crystal combat, and traditional Pot PvP (Nodebuff).

    Head to our Registration page and complete the 3-step wizard to have your Minecraft IGN and Discord tag reviewed by an official tier tester.

    Players may use Vanilla Minecraft, Lunar Client, Badlion Client, or Feather Client with standard HUD mods. Any macro, autoclicker, or reach modification is strictly prohibited.
