Official Disciplines

  • ⚔️ Sword (1.9+) — Classic sword duels requiring precise spacing, timing, and sweep aim.
  • 🪓 Axe PvP — Heavy combat utilizing shields and axes, focusing on critical hits and shield breaks.
  • 🔮 Crystal PvP — Fast-paced end crystal and obsidian placement combat inside lethal blast zones.
  • 🧪 Pot PvP — Speed-focused duels using instant health and regeneration splash potions.

Tournament Regulations & Fair Play:

• Ping Equalization: Matches are hosted on neutral Frankfurt & Virginia proxy nodes to guarantee balanced knockback.

• Client Verification: Allowed clients are Lunar, Badlion, and Vanilla. Custom autoclickers, reach macros, or ghost clients lead to immediate permanent ban.

• Tier Test Protocol: First to 5 wins against an official tier examiner determines your official HT1, LT1, HT2, or LT2 ranking.
